ServoMap shows what fuel costs at every reporting station near you, cheapest first, from the official state price feeds.

The map
The cheapest stations are tagged with their price and brand; the rest are coloured dots by price. Pull the sheet up for the full list, or down to see the whole map.

Trends
The state average for your fuel over 30 or 90 days, the cheapest day of the week, how far prices spread nearby, and which brands sit under the local average.

Your log
Record each fill-up and see what you spent this month, six months of spending, and how much you saved against the local average when you filled.

Saved stations
Keep the stations you use and see how each price moved since you last looked. Price-drop alerts can be switched on now and will arrive once they launch.

Widgets and Siri
Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show the cheapest price for your fuel. Ask Siri for the cheapest fuel nearby.

Fuels: U91, E10, U95, U98 and Diesel.
Coverage: New South Wales and Western Australia today, with more states as their feeds are connected.

Prices come from government reporting schemes and refresh every 15 minutes. Always check the price at the pump.

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your saved stations and fill-ups stay on your iPhone.
