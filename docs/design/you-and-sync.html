You, your car, and alerts that know you

Saved stations and the fill-up log move behind an avatar. You can sign in with Apple or Google so they follow you between devices. Your car is picked from a shared catalogue that fills in the tank size, and notifications are written about your stations, your car and your habits. Four decisions below need your call before anything is built.

Navigation: three tabs and an avatar

The sheet keeps the three things everyone uses: Nearby, Trends and Search. Everything that is yours moves to an avatar in the top-right of the map, as the App Store and Apple Maps do.

  • The avatar shows your initials, or a fuel-pump glyph before you sign in. A dot on it means an alert is waiting.
  • Tapping it opens You, a full-height sheet with four pages under one segmented bar: Saved · Log · Car · Alerts.
  • Signing in is optional. Without it everything works and stays on this iPhone. Signing in later merges what is on the phone into your account.

Sign-in and sync

CloudKit stores data against the iPhone's iCloud account. A Google sign-in cannot open that data, and the website cannot read it without a separate Apple sign-in. So "CloudKit" and "sign in with Google" pull in different directions. The three ways to square them:

OptionApple sign-inGoogle sign-inSyncs to the websiteServer can send personal alertsCostWork
A. CloudKit onlyAutomatic (iCloud)Not possibleNoOnly by also copying prices into CloudKitFree2 days
B. ServoMap accounts (recommended)YesYesYesYes, it already knows your stationsFree tier (Cloudflare D1)4–5 days
C. CloudKit + sign-in for alertsYesFor alerts onlyNoYesFree5–6 days, two stores to keep in step

Recommendation: B. The Worker gains /api/v1/me/* routes. It verifies the Apple or Google identity token itself and keeps your saved stations, fill-ups, car and alert settings in Cloudflare D1. The same account then works on the website, and the alert job already knows whose stations to watch. App Store rule 4.8 is met because Sign in with Apple is offered next to Google. Sign-in is optional, and an account can be deleted from the Alerts page, which rule 5.1.1 requires.

My car: pick it, don't type it

A shared vehicle catalogue, the same for every user, served by the Worker at /api/v1/vehicles so the website can use it too.

  • Search first: type "corolla" or "ranger" and pick from make · model · years. Or step through Make → Model → Year → Variant menus.
  • The variant fills in the tank size and the fuel it takes. Both stay editable; an edited value shows "your value" with a link back to the catalogue figure.
  • A side view of the car sits at the top of the page. It is one of about eight body shapes (hatch, sedan, wagon, SUV, ute, van, coupe, people mover) that we draw, tinted in the colour you pick. Manufacturer photos are copyrighted and vary in style, so they are not used.
  • The car drives the app: the default fuel, the cost of a full tank on every station page, and the fill-up reminder.

Catalogue data. Australia's Green Vehicle Guide lists makes, models, variants and fuel types, but not tank size. Tank sizes would come from manufacturer spec sheets for the 150 or so best-selling models, each with its source recorded. Unlisted cars can still be entered by hand. The data source is decision 2 below.

Alerts written for you

Each alert names your station, your car or your habit, and says what to do. At most one a day, never during quiet hours (10 pm to 7 am by default), and each kind has its own switch.

AlertFires whenSent from
Price dropA saved station drops by 3¢ or moreServer, after each 15-minute ingest
Fill up soonYou usually fill every N days and it is day N − 1; names the cheapest station near homeiPhone, from your log
Cycle lowThe average near home is in the bottom 15% of its 60-day rangeServer, daily
Monthly summaryFirst day of the month: spent, saved, and litresiPhone

Price-drop and cycle alerts need the server to know your saved stations and home area, which is why they depend on option B or C. The other two work with no account.

Build order

Phase 1 · about 1 day

The You sheet

Avatar, three tabs, and Saved · Log · Car · Alerts as pages. Log gets Overview and Fill-ups sub-pages. Everything still on the phone.

Phase 2 · 2–3 days

Car catalogue

Vehicle data with sources, /api/v1/vehicles in the Worker and OpenAPI, search and menus, body-shape drawings, tank auto-fill with override.

Phase 3 · 4–5 days

Accounts and sync

Sign in with Apple and Google, D1 schema, /api/v1/me routes, merge on first sign-in, account deletion. Website sign-in follows.

Phase 4 · 2 days

Alerts

Device alerts first. Then the server job after ingest sends APNs pushes with the team key already in 1Password.

Decisions needed

  1. Sync: B (ServoMap accounts with Apple and Google), A (CloudKit only, no Google), or C (both)?
  2. Car data: curate tank sizes for about 150 models from manufacturer spec sheets, or buy a licensed vehicle dataset?
  3. Car picture: our body-shape drawings tinted by colour, or no picture?
  4. Alerts: which of the four to ship first?
