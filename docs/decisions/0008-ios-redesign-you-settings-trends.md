# 0008 — iOS redesign: You, the car, Settings, Filters and Trends

- Date: 2026-09-30
- Status: **accepted**
- Owner: Henry Chen
- Evidence: design canvas "ServoMap iOS redesign" (https://claude.ai/artifact/4bAdUCxpBgytkx2oz4nnNk),
  16 artboards drawn with the owner over several review rounds on 2026-09-30

## Problem

The owner judged the You sheet "not designed at all" and the car drawing sloppy; adding a car had no
designed flow; Settings, Filters and Trends were thin or missing. Checked against
[`docs/design/system.md`](../design/system.md), the You sheet broke its own rules: every block was a
card ("no card soup"), the month figures were a KPI tile row, and cards used an 18 pt radius outside
the 2 / 4 / 6 scale. The custom bottom bar also fell short of the system look and motion.

## Options considered

- Settings list style: iOS grouped cards; 素 plain list with Mincho group titles; a named reference
  app. Tried panels first; the owner found them worse.
- Row icons: tinted tiles (rejected as ugly), content previews, plain line icons.
- Car pictures: parametric side profiles drawn per model from real dimensions; photos from Wikimedia
  (mixed angles, attribution); AI-generated studio renders in one style.
- Year choice: every model year as a grid; one choice per generation, shown as a year range.

## Choice

- **Navigation:** the system `TabView` with the Liquid Glass tab bar: Nearby, Trends, Settings and a
  Search tab in the search role. The avatar on the map still opens You.
- **List style (every screen):** 素 plain list. 24 pt gutters, Mincho group titles, 56 pt rows at
  17 pt, a 0.5 pt hairline only between rows and inset to the text, no cards around groups. Settings
  rows carry plain line icons with no tile behind them.
- **Settings:** a root list whose rows show their current value and open second-level pages:
  Car, Directions (Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze, only apps installed, plus "Ask each time"),
  Map (default fuel, compare radius with a live mini map, old prices, members-only), Alerts (rules,
  home, quiet hours as a 24 h strip), Appearance, Your data, Data sources, Version.
- **You:** identity, the car as the one picture on the page, a ruled "Full tank now" row, the month
  as a ruled figure row with its fill-ups, saved stations as ledger rows on one price scale.
- **Adding a car:** Make (1:1 marks) → Model (one row per model, tank range across generations) →
  Years (one card per generation, shown as a year range; skipped when a model has one generation) →
  Details (name, fuel, tank with catalogue value and override). No paint choice.
- **Car pictures:** one AI-generated studio render per catalogue generation: white car, front
  three-quarter view facing left, blank plates, the maker's badge kept as on the real car (owner's
  choice, after the model kept drawing badges when told not to), white background placed on paper with
  multiply. A generation without its own render shows an unbranded stand-in for its body type. Generated by `design/car-renders/generate.py`; every image's model, prompt, seed and review
  are recorded beside it. Shown as an illustration, not a photo of the actual vehicle.
- **Car make marks:** Simple Icons (CC0 files; the marks stay their owners' trademarks), ink on a
  white 1:1 tile; makes without one keep a monogram tile, as in decision 0003.
- **Filters:** order, brands as a 1:1 logo grid, a price histogram of the stations in view coloured
  by tier, reported-within as dots on a scale, members-only.
- **Trends:** four sub-pages. Overview (averages and cheapest by state; small multiples since June),
  States (band chart with the data gap shown, every fuel on one scale), Cities (range plot by city,
  one city's price distribution; history "collecting since 30 Sep"), Timing (change heatmap once
  four weeks are collected; share of stations reporting today).

## Consequences

- Car renders cost about US$0.05 each through fal (flux-2-max), roughly US$9 for the catalogue, and
  need a person to check each one before it ships. Badges identify the vehicle and imply no endorsement;
  the pictures are labelled as illustrations.
- City history and the timing heatmap depend on daily roll-ups in the price-history database
  (decision 0006); until they exist those charts show a collecting state.
- The previous CarSilhouette drawings and the paint picker are removed.
